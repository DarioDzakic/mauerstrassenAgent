### Portfolio Strategy & Caveats

The overall strategy is rooted in **relative strength and technical momentum** rather than fundamental valuation. By limiting individual position sizes to **12.5% (31,250 EUR)** of your total 250,000 EUR equity allocation, we mitigate the risk of a single earnings disappointment or sector-specific pullback. The **50% cash buffer** is the most critical component of this plan; it provides the liquidity necessary to exit or rotate positions quickly without forcing the liquidation of your entire portfolio during a market downturn. 

**Concentration Risk Warning:** This portfolio is heavily concentrated in the **AI Infrastructure and Energy-Power nexus**. While these sectors are currently exhibiting the strongest momentum, they are correlated. If the AI investment cycle cools or energy prices experience a sudden global supply shock, your entire equity portfolio may face significant simultaneous drawdowns. **Strict adherence to the 5-7% stop-loss protocol is non-negotiable.** This portfolio is designed for an active trader; if you are unable to monitor these positions at least weekly, you should transition toward a more diversified and passive asset allocation.
