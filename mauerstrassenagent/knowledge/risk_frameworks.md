# Risk Frameworks

## Risk Tolerance Levels

### Conservative
- Goal: capital preservation with modest growth; minimize drawdowns
- Max equity allocation: 40–60%
- Preferred strategies: dividend investing, value investing, index/passive
- Typical sectors: consumer staples, utilities, healthcare, financials (banks/insurance)
- Avoid: speculative micro-caps, high-momentum names, leveraged positions
- Expected annual return range: 4–7%
- Max acceptable drawdown: 10–15%

### Moderate
- Goal: balanced growth and income; accept short-term volatility for long-term gains
- Max equity allocation: 60–80%
- Preferred strategies: growth, value, dividend blend
- All sectors appropriate with proper diversification
- Limit individual position sizes to 5–8% of portfolio
- Expected annual return range: 7–12%
- Max acceptable drawdown: 15–25%

### Aggressive
- Goal: maximize capital appreciation; willing to accept significant drawdowns
- Max equity allocation: 80–100%
- Preferred strategies: growth, momentum, concentrated bets
- Comfortable with high-growth sectors: technology, biotech, emerging markets
- Can hold concentrated positions (up to 10–15% per stock) with conviction
- Expected annual return range: 12–20%+
- Max acceptable drawdown: 25–40%

## Investment Horizon Guidelines

### Short-term (< 1 year)
- Focus on momentum and tactical trades
- Higher cash allocation as buffer
- Avoid illiquid or highly volatile positions unless aggressive profile
- Consider: large-cap momentum, sector rotation plays

### Mid-term (1–5 years)
- Best window for growth and value strategies to compound
- Can ride through 1–2 market corrections
- Diversify across 3–5 sectors minimum
- Rebalance quarterly or semi-annually

### Long-term (5+ years)
- Full compounding benefit; can afford to ride out bear markets
- Ideal for dividend reinvestment and growth strategies
- Broader diversification including international exposure
- Less need for tactical trading; focus on quality holdings

## Portfolio Construction Rules
- No single stock > 15% of total portfolio (aggressive) or > 8% (conservative)
- Minimum 3 sectors represented for diversification
- Total allocation must equal 100% — remainder in cash or bonds
- Rebalance when any position drifts > 5% from target weight
- Flag concentration risk when > 40% in a single sector

## Common Profile Contradictions to Flag
- Aggressive risk tolerance + short horizon (< 6 months) → high chance of realizing losses
- Conservative risk tolerance + growth-only strategy → mismatch in volatility expectations
- Large capital + no diversification → unnecessary concentration risk
- Short horizon + illiquid investments → liquidity mismatch
