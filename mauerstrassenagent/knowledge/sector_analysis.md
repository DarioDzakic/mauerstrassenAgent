# Sector Analysis

## Technology
- Drivers: AI/ML adoption, cloud migration, cybersecurity demand, semiconductor cycle
- Risk factors: regulatory scrutiny (antitrust, data privacy), high valuations, cyclical capex
- Suitable for: growth and momentum strategies; moderate-to-aggressive risk profiles
- Key sub-sectors: cloud infrastructure, AI/ML platforms, cybersecurity, semiconductors, SaaS

## Healthcare
- Drivers: aging demographics, biotech innovation, GLP-1/obesity drugs, medical devices
- Risk factors: drug trial failures, pricing regulation, patent cliffs
- Suitable for: growth and dividend strategies; moderate risk profiles
- Key sub-sectors: pharmaceuticals, biotech, medical devices, healthcare IT, managed care

## Energy
- Drivers: energy transition, oil supply constraints, LNG demand, grid modernization
- Risk factors: commodity price volatility, regulatory shifts, stranded asset risk
- Suitable for: value and dividend strategies; moderate-to-aggressive profiles
- Key sub-sectors: oil & gas majors, renewables, utilities, energy infrastructure

## Financials
- Drivers: interest rate environment, credit cycle, fintech disruption, capital markets activity
- Risk factors: credit losses in downturns, regulatory capital requirements, rate sensitivity
- Suitable for: value and dividend strategies; moderate risk profiles
- Key sub-sectors: banks, insurance, asset management, fintech, REITs

## Consumer Discretionary
- Drivers: consumer confidence, e-commerce growth, luxury demand, experiential spending
- Risk factors: recession sensitivity, input cost inflation, shifting consumer preferences
- Suitable for: growth and momentum strategies; moderate-to-aggressive profiles
- Key sub-sectors: retail, e-commerce, automotive, travel & leisure, luxury goods

## Consumer Staples
- Drivers: stable demand, pricing power, emerging market growth
- Risk factors: margin pressure from input costs, private label competition
- Suitable for: dividend and value strategies; conservative profiles
- Key sub-sectors: food & beverage, household products, personal care, tobacco

## Industrials
- Drivers: infrastructure spending, reshoring/nearshoring, defense budgets, automation
- Risk factors: economic cycle sensitivity, supply chain disruptions, labor costs
- Suitable for: value and growth strategies; moderate profiles
- Key sub-sectors: aerospace & defense, machinery, construction, transportation

## Sector Rotation Patterns
- Early cycle (recovery): consumer discretionary, financials, industrials outperform
- Mid cycle (expansion): technology, industrials continue strength
- Late cycle (slowdown): energy, materials, healthcare tend to hold up
- Recession: consumer staples, utilities, healthcare are defensive havens
- Monitor leading indicators (PMI, yield curve, credit spreads) for rotation signals
