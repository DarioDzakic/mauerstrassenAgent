# Investment Strategies

## Value Investing
- Focus on stocks trading below their intrinsic value based on fundamental analysis
- Key metrics: P/E ratio below sector average, P/B < 1.5, strong free cash flow
- Best suited for: moderate-to-conservative risk profiles, mid-to-long horizons (3–10+ years)
- Classic approach: buy quality companies at a discount, hold until fair value is reached
- Watch for value traps — cheap stocks that are cheap for a reason (declining business, structural headwinds)

## Growth Investing
- Target companies with above-average revenue and earnings growth rates
- Key metrics: revenue growth > 15% YoY, expanding margins, high reinvestment rate
- Best suited for: moderate-to-aggressive risk profiles, mid-to-long horizons (3–10+ years)
- Prioritize market leaders in expanding industries with durable competitive advantages
- Accept higher valuations (P/E 30–60+) if justified by sustained growth trajectory

## Momentum Investing
- Ride stocks showing strong recent price and earnings momentum
- Key metrics: 6-month and 12-month relative strength, earnings surprise history, volume trends
- Best suited for: aggressive risk profiles, short-to-mid horizons (3 months – 2 years)
- Requires disciplined exit strategy — set trailing stops, respect trend reversals
- Higher turnover and transaction costs; not suitable for passive or conservative investors

## Dividend Investing
- Build a portfolio of stocks paying reliable and growing dividends
- Key metrics: dividend yield > 2%, payout ratio < 70%, dividend growth streak > 5 years
- Best suited for: conservative risk profiles, long horizons (5+ years), income-focused goals
- Reinvesting dividends compounds returns over time (DRIP strategy)
- Sectors with strong dividend histories: utilities, consumer staples, healthcare, REITs

## Index / Passive Strategy
- Match market returns through broad index exposure (S&P 500, total market, international)
- Lowest cost, lowest effort, historically beats most active managers over 10+ year periods
- Best suited for: any risk profile, especially those without time for active management
- Can tilt toward factors (value, small-cap, quality) for enhanced returns
